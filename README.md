## Why this kit


In Siteline’s “State of Subcontractor Billing in 2026” survey of 492 construction finance and operations professionals (reported by Contractor Magazine, September 4, 2026):


- 92% of subcontractors floated payroll from their own pockets while waiting for payment.
- 43% waited 90 days or more to collect final payment plus retainage.
- 56% missed a critical mechanic’s lien deadline in the past two years.
- 67% spend 11 or more hours every month preparing, submitting, and tracking pay applications.


This kit does not change what GCs owe you. It changes the four things you control: whether your pay application is clean enough to approve without a round trip, whether your retainage has a release date on the calendar, whether your lien deadlines are watched, and whether your cash-flow gap is known before payroll day.


## The 7 sheets


1. **Setup** — company, default retainage %, pay-app cycle day, and your project list.
2. **Schedule of Values** — line items per project: contract value, % complete, earned to date, stored materials, retainage withheld.
3. **Pay Applications** — per-period pay apps: gross earned, stored materials, retainage withheld, amount due, status, days outstanding, open total.
4. **Retainage Tracker** — retainage held per project, release trigger, trigger date, release due date, days overdue, status.
5. **Lien Deadlines** — USER-ENTERED deadlines with countdown and alerts: ON TRACK, DUE WITHIN 30 DAYS, DUE WITHIN 14 DAYS, URGENT — 7 DAYS OR LESS, OVERDUE.
6. **Cash-Flow Gap** — expected receipts vs payroll due dates: float gap and weeks of payroll at risk.
7. **Pay-App Checklist** — 12 pre-submit checks with an automatic READY / NOT READY verdict.
